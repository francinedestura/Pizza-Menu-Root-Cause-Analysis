Reading the weekly trend. Recent weeks only include tickets that have already closed — Wk of Aug 17 and Aug 24 will grow as open tickets close, so a drop at the right edge is not yet proof of improvement. The "% of tagged" view is the fairer read since tagging coverage also shifts week to week. Execution error = SOP miss pre-live/go-live (menu opportunity). Data error = bad or missing client info at intake (OSM/DATA opportunity) — never combined into one "error rate." Not tagged (placeholder) and no-field tickets are always reported explicitly.
