Reading the trend. Recent dates only include tickets that have already closed — the right edge will grow as open tickets close, so treat it as provisional. The "% of tagged" view is the fairer read since tagging coverage also shifts over time. Execution error = SOP miss pre-live/go-live (menu opportunity). Data error = bad or missing client info at intake (OSM/DATA opportunity) — never combined into one "error rate." Not tagged (placeholder) and no-field tickets are always reported explicitly.
